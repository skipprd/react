# Product Hunt Launch Checklist

---

## T-minus 2 Weeks

- [ ] Finalise PH listing: tagline, short description, full description, topics
- [ ] Create or commission gallery images (6 slides per briefs.md)
- [ ] Record and edit demo video (75–90 seconds)
- [ ] Write maker comment draft — review with team
- [ ] Identify 30–50 supporters: friends, colleagues, community members, fellow makers who genuinely care about data tooling
- [ ] Send pre-launch DMs to inner circle (see supporter-dm.md)
- [ ] Draft all social posts (Twitter thread, LinkedIn, supporter tweets)
- [ ] Set up PH listing as draft (do NOT publish yet)
- [ ] Choose launch day: Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday
- [ ] Confirm: who will be online for the full 24 hours?

---

## T-minus 1 Week

- [ ] Send pre-launch DMs to broader supporter list
- [ ] Tease on Twitter/X: "Something's coming next [DAY]..." — no link yet
- [ ] Post a LinkedIn teaser: problem-focused (the pain of data setup), no product mention yet
- [ ] Review FAQ responses — assign team members to common question categories
- [ ] Test the product flow end-to-end: make sure `skippr init` → `skippr run` works cleanly for the demo scenario
- [ ] Prepare a "known issues" doc internally so you can respond honestly to bugs reported on launch day
- [ ] Schedule a team standup for 11:45 PM PT the night before launch

---

## T-minus 1 Day

- [ ] Final review of PH listing (check for typos, broken links, image order)
- [ ] Upload gallery images and video to PH draft
- [ ] Pre-write the maker comment in a text file — ready to paste
- [ ] Notify supporters: "We go live at midnight PT. I'll send the link first thing."
- [ ] Prepare browser tabs: PH dashboard, Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit, email
- [ ] Get sleep. Seriously. The next 24 hours are a marathon.

---

## Launch Day (Hour by Hour)

### 12:00 AM PT — Go Live

- [ ] Publish the PH listing
- [ ] Immediately post the maker comment
- [ ] Send launch DM to all supporters with the direct PH link
- [ ] Post the main Twitter launch tweet + thread
- [ ] Post the LinkedIn announcement

### 12:00–2:00 AM PT — Early Momentum

- [ ] Respond to every PH comment within 10 minutes
- [ ] Monitor for early bugs or questions — fix what you can, acknowledge what you can't
- [ ] Retweet/boost supporter tweets
- [ ] Check PH ranking — you want top 5 by the 2-hour mark

### 6:00 AM PT — Morning Push

- [ ] Post in relevant Slack communities (dbt Slack, data engineering groups, local tech Slack)
- [ ] Post on Reddit: r/dataengineering, r/analytics (genuine, not spammy — share the story, not just the link)
- [ ] Send a reminder tweet: "We're live on PH today — here's what we built and why" with a fresh angle
- [ ] Respond to all new PH comments

### 12:00 PM PT — Midday

- [ ] Post a midday update tweet: "X hours in, here's what we're hearing..." with a quote from a commenter
- [ ] Continue responding to every PH comment
- [ ] If ranking has slipped, send a targeted reminder to engaged supporters
- [ ] Share on any newsletters or email lists you have access to

### 6:00 PM PT — Afternoon Push

- [ ] Final social push: "Last few hours on Product Hunt — thanks for the incredible feedback"
- [ ] Engage with any late comments — these are often the most thoughtful
- [ ] Start drafting a "day 1 learnings" post for tomorrow

### 11:59 PM PT — Day Ends

- [ ] Take a breath. The 24-hour window is closed.

---

## Post-Launch (Days 2–7)

- [ ] Write a Twitter/LinkedIn recap: "We launched on PH. Here's what happened." Include numbers (upvotes, comments, ranking, signups)
- [ ] Respond to any remaining PH comments — engagement doesn't stop at midnight
- [ ] Follow up with everyone who commented: thank them, answer follow-ups
- [ ] Publish a blog post / maker diary about the launch experience
- [ ] Share learnings internally: what worked, what didn't, what to do differently
- [ ] Track conversions: PH visitors → site visits → signups → active users
- [ ] Add "Featured on Product Hunt" badge to site if you placed well

---

## Golden Rules for Launch Day

1. **Respond to every comment.** Every single one. Within 15 minutes if possible.
2. **Never ask for upvotes.** Ask for feedback. Ask for opinions. Ask what connectors people want.
3. **Be authentic.** Acknowledge limitations. Share what's next. People vote for makers, not just products.
4. **Stagger your social posts.** Don't dump everything at midnight. Spread across the day to capture different time zones.
5. **Have fun.** If you're not enjoying it, it'll show.
