# Supporter Outreach DM Template

Send 1–2 weeks before launch to warm up supporters. Personalise the opening line.

---

## Pre-Launch DM (send 5–7 days before)

> Hey [NAME],
>
> Quick heads up — we're launching Skippr on Product Hunt on [DATE].
>
> The short version: it's an AI Data Agent for turning raw source data into AI-ready warehouse assets. Local-first CLI, standard dbt output, and a lot of focus on trust around schema handling and data flow.
>
> No pressure at all, but if you have a minute on launch day to take a look and leave honest feedback, that would mean a lot.

---

## Launch Day DM (send at launch, ~12:05 AM PT)

> Hey [NAME], we're live!
>
> Here's the Product Hunt link: [PH_LINK]
>
> If it's relevant to you, we'd especially value a real comment on the positioning, trust model, or connector story. No pressure either way — thanks for the support.

---

## Key Rules

- **Never say "please upvote"** — PH actively penalises coordinated voting. Ask for feedback and engagement.
- **Personalise** — Reference something specific ("saw your post about dbt last week" or "knowing you deal with Snowflake daily").
- **Keep it short** — Respect people's time. One paragraph max per message.
- **Don't blast** — Send to people who genuinely would find the product relevant, not a mass list.
- **Time it right** — Pre-launch DMs 5–7 days out. Launch DMs within the first 30 minutes.
