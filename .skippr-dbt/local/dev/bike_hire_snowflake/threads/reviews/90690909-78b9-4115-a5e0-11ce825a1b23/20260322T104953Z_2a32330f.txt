Blocking correctness issues remain:
1. `fct_trip_event` / `fct_trip`: `fct_trip_event` filters out all rows where `trip IS NULL`, but the approved contract allows null `trip_id` events and `fct_trip` depends on them for `start_anchored` and `orphan_end` construction. `fct_trip` also only builds grouped `trip_id` rows, hard-codes `trip_grain_type = 'trip_id'`, and uses `event_name` predicates like `trip_start`/`trip_end` instead of the specified canonical values `start`/`end`/`pause`/`resume`. This will drop valid events and produce incorrect trip rows, counts, and statuses. Remove the null-trip filter from `fct_trip_event` (while keeping the timestamp filter), implement the required three construction paths in `fct_trip`, and align `event_name` and `trip_grain_type` logic to the spec.
2. `agg_rider_daily_trip_activity`: business KPI fields (`trip_count`, `distinct_bike_count`, `total_trip_duration_seconds`, `avg_trip_duration_seconds`) are computed over all `fct_trip` rows instead of only `trip_status IN ('completed','in_progress')`. This mixes anomalous trips into rider-day KPIs. Restrict those metrics to the required statuses and leave `anomaly_trip_count` as the non-business-status count.
3. `agg_bike_daily_utilization`: the same status-filter defect exists for `trip_count`, `distinct_rider_count`, `total_trip_duration_seconds`, and `avg_trip_duration_seconds`; `anomaly_trip_count` is derived from `data_quality_flag <> 'ok'` instead of `trip_status NOT IN ('completed','in_progress')`; and `SUM(trip_duration_seconds)` is not coalesced to 0, so all-null groups can return null. Update the KPI aggregations to follow the spec, derive anomaly counts from `trip_status`, and coalesce total duration to 0.
